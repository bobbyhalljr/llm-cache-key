## Limitations and blockers
No destination remains blocked. This is a synthetic cache-key fixture with a local Map. It makes no model call and measures no latency or savings. No production authentication, concurrency, distributed invalidation or live revocation was tested. The caller must supply trusted identity, current revisions, cacheability and reliable time. Only the narrow text request contract is supported. A hash does not authorize access or encrypt cached data.
